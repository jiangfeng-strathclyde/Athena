## Reconciliation and bounded conclusions

**C1 — contradicted within the inspected corpus.** E2 reports a relevant counterexample to “always”: a higher AngleStep mean in an obstructed corridor. E1/E4 provide a favorable clear-corridor case, which cannot rescue that universal claim. The observed directions differ across different corridor conditions and separately collected datasets; this is not a demonstrated disagreement about the same experiment. Those differences are plausible explanations to investigate, not an established causal explanation for the reversal. E3 supplies no directional result, and E5/E6 supply no study content. Support for lower AngleStep error in obstructed corridors was **not found in the inspected corpus**. The evidence supports the narrower wording: “The supplied clear-corridor experiment reports lower mean angular error for AngleStep, whereas the supplied obstructed-corridor experiment reports higher mean angular error.” This follows E1/E2 without averaging the results or counting sources as votes.

**C2 — supported as the supplied experiment's descriptive report.** E7 directly supplies the two reported means, and E10 repeats that same experiment without independent validation. Direct counterevidence to that specific clear-corridor report was **not found in the inspected corpus**. E8 is outside its condition and E9 lacks the information needed to connect it to that experiment. The defensible wording is: “In S preprint v1's reported clear-corridor test, AngleStep's mean angular error was 2°, compared with 4° for Grid.” This does not establish a statistically significant effect, reproducibility, population-level improvement, or performance in other corridors.

The decisive missing checks are the complete S and F methods/results, sample sizes, uncertainty estimates and comparable evaluation definitions; U's effect estimates, uncertainty, power and corridor conditions; usable content for M; and J's results plus evidence establishing its relationship to S. Such material could qualify the interpretation or expose a correction, but no missing result is assumed. Confirming fixture identities would be separate from testing claim support, and validating reported numbers against raw artifacts would be a separate numerical audit. No broader literature search or systematic-review completeness is claimed.
