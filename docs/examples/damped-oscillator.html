Question 3

Which claims survive inspection?

Every conclusion remains bounded by the declared equation and conditions.

Supported · energy does not increase

E=½mv²+½kx² and dE/dt=−cv²≤0 for c≥0. The sampled check below exposes the same property.

Refuted · displacement always decreases

An underdamped trace crosses zero. Its envelope and energy decay, but signed displacement is not monotone.

Bounded · “more damping is faster”

Overdamping can lengthen return time. This 2% probe is useful here, not a universal optimality proof.
